Welcome to EduHub

EduHub is your student productivity workspace for timetable planning, PDF summarization, and AI-powered homework support.

Run the app from the server with npm start and open http://localhost:5173.

Why EduHub?

  • 📚 Smart timetable planning for Monday–Friday school weeks.
  • 🧠 AI study recommendations based on your schedule.
  • 📝 PDF textbook summarizer with TL;DR, notes, and flashcards.
  • 🎓 Friendly assignment tutoring that explains concepts step-by-step.
Quick start: Signup, then open the dashboard to start using EduHub.
